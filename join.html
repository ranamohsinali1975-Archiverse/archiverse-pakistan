The first question

What is the one skill or lesson your architecture education did not give you?

Tell us your university or firm, the year you graduated, and one gap you have felt. Bring an example if you can. The answers will shape the forum's first survey and its first published results.

Take the 5-minute survey

Anonymous unless you choose to be contacted. You can also answer on any Archiverse-Pakistan post, or on the LinkedIn article that started this conversation.

Ground rules

  1. Respect people. Criticise the system, the decision, the outcome. Not the person.
  2. Back claims with evidence. A document, a number, a dated example. Say when something is your experience or your opinion.
  3. Bring a solution. Or at least the next question. A complaint without a direction is not yet a contribution.
