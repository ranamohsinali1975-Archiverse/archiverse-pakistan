About

An independent forum for the future of architecture in Pakistan

Archiverse-Pakistan brings students, educators, practitioners and institutions together to identify gaps in architecture education and practice, test them with evidence, and push for solutions that serve Pakistan and stand up globally.

Pakistan's architecture was born with the nation in 1947. By 2047 it will be 100 years old. This forum exists to ask, openly and with evidence, what we will hand over by then.

Three values

  1. Evidence before opinion. Every claim is labelled as a verified fact with its source, a community experience, or an opinion. If we cannot verify something, it stays an opinion or it is cut.
  2. Criticism with a solution. We criticise systems, decisions and outcomes, not individuals. Every critical piece ends with a proposed solution or a question that moves the debate forward.
  3. Every voice counts. From student to principal. Institutions are invited to share their position, and we publish their replies. We publish only verified facts; see our Standards.

What we are not

Not an anti-institution campaign. Not a firm's marketing page. Not a place for personal attacks. We never publish a serious allegation about a named institution or person without a second, independent check.

Who runs it

Archiverse-Pakistan is founder-led by Rana Mohsin Ali, an architect writing on education, institutions and practice in Pakistan. The forum is community-first: its direction is set by what students, graduates, educators and practitioners say is missing.

How to take part

Answer the questions, take the surveys, join the debates, send your proposals and evidence, and — if you represent an institution — respond on record. See Join.
