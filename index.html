Why this forum exists

Pakistan's architecture profession turns 100 in 2047. What will we hand over by then?

01

Evidence before opinion

Every claim we publish is labelled: a verified fact with its source, a community experience, or an opinion. If we cannot verify it, we say so.

02

Criticism with a solution

We criticise systems, decisions and outcomes, not people. Every critical piece ends with a proposed solution or a question that moves it forward.

03

Every voice counts

From a first-year student to a department head, a site engineer to a registrar. Institutions are invited to respond, and we correct our mistakes in the open.

The horizon

Measurable progress, not just posts

The point of this forum is to turn debate into something that can be measured: survey results published, proposals tested with willing departments and firms, standards reviewed, institutions responding on record.

2026–2027ListenAn evidence-backed map of the gaps in education and practice, built with the community and published openly.
2027–2030ProposeConcrete proposals on curriculum, accreditation, building control and climate practice, tested in pilots.
2030–2040AdoptProposals adopted by institutions; yearly benchmarks against global peers.
2040–2047StandPakistani architecture education and practice recognised as globally comparable on named measures.
