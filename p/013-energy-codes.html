S12 · STANDARDS · EP 07

Islamabad has decided every new building must meet an energy code

Standards that Save Lives · Episode 07 · ASHRAE 90.1, IECC and India's ECBC beside Pakistan's ECBC-2023

01THE BASICS

Where does a building's heat and energy go?

Heat crosses the envelope three ways

Conduction

Through walls and roof. U-value, W/m²K: watts per m² per kelvin of difference.

Convection

Air leaking through gaps, and air moving heat off warm surfaces.

Radiation

Sun through glass. SHGC: the share of solar heat a window lets in.

Then the energy goes four ways

Envelope

Sets the load the rest must handle.

HVAC

Removes the heat the envelope let in.

Lighting

Lighting power density, in W/m².

Plug loads

Computers and appliances; heat too.

Energy codes regulate each layer, envelope first: every watt kept out is a watt the cooling plant never removes.

07THE STANDARDS

What the codes require and save

  1. ASHRAE 90.1 (USA)

    All buildings except low-rise homes. Current edition 90.1-2025; updated every three years.

  2. US DOE determination

    90.1-2022 vs 2019: 9.8% site energy, 8.9% energy cost, 9.3% carbon (national average).

  3. IECC (USA)

    The ICC's model energy code. In 2023 PEC, NEECA and ICC agreed to update Pakistan's code on the 2021 IECC.

  4. ECBC 2017 (India)

    Commercial buildings ≥ 100 kW load or > 120 kVA demand. Tiers: ECBC 25%, ECBC+ 35%, SuperECBC 50%.

08SHADE BEFORE YOU COOL

Infosys SDB-1: envelope first, then 34% less HVAC energy

Hyderabad, India (peak 46 °C): 30% glazing, insulated walls with thermal breaks, lightshelves that double as sunshades. Shown: brise-soleil louvres on another building.

09BEST PRACTICE

Two Indian buildings, measured

INFOSYS SDB-1

  • Hyderabad · 23,226 m² · occupied Feb 2011
  • 95% daylight autonomy, no direct sun
  • Radiant half: 34% less HVAC energy than the optimised VAV half, at slightly lower cost

PARYAVARAN BHAWAN

  • New Delhi · completed 2013 · CPWD
  • AAC + rockwool walls, double glazing, chilled beams, 930 kWp PV
  • 24.13 kWh/m²/yr: 67.3% below the GRIHA benchmark
10PAKISTAN · KARACHI

Habib University: shade and less glass, rated EDGE Advanced

External shading and a lower window-to-wall ratio. Assessed energy savings: 40% (EDGE) to 48% (Habib University, citing the 2025 EDGE assessment).

12PAKISTAN TODAY

What ECBC-2023 already gives us

  1. Homes included

    Applies to residential and commercial buildings.

  2. Envelope limits

    Roof U ≤ 0.44, external wall U ≤ 0.57 W/m²K.

  3. Free tools

    BERC, UET Peshawar: ECBC-2023 Compliance Checking Tool, Building Load Calculation Tool.

  4. Islamabad

    CDA: mandatory for all new construction; existing buildings in phases after financial evaluation.

  5. Critics' view

    Dawn op-ed (2024): leans on ASHRAE material specs; passive rules skip tall, commercial and office buildings; too few energy auditors.

13PAKISTAN VS BEST PRACTICE

Where the next gains are

PAKISTAN TODAY

  • Applied through authority by-laws: Islamabad for new buildings (CDA, 2025)
  • Free compliance tools from BERC, UET Peshawar
  • Envelope limits for roofs and walls, homes included

BEST PRACTICE

  • USA: each 90.1 edition gets a federal savings figure: 9.8% (DOE)
  • India: ECBC+ and SuperECBC reward 35% and 50% savings
  • Infosys SDB-1: savings metered side by side
14A PROPOSAL FOR DISCUSSION

Three moves, each with a precedent

  1. One sheet with every plan

    Each authority asks for the compliance-tool printout, as CDA now requires ECBC for new Islamabad buildings.

  2. Publish measured savings

    A Pakistani savings determination for ECBC-2023, as the US DOE publishes for each 90.1.

  3. A ladder above the minimum

    Voluntary higher tiers, like India's ECBC+ and SuperECBC.

  4. Limits

    Needs trained plan reviewers and energy auditors, and metered Pakistani data before any tier is set.

15PRACTITIONER CHECKLIST

On your next plan

  1. Check the by-law

    Which authority, and is ECBC-2023 compliance required?

  2. Run the tool early

    BERC's ECBC-2023 Compliance Checking Tool at concept stage.

  3. Roof and walls

    Insulate to U ≤ 0.44 (roof) and ≤ 0.57 W/m²K (walls).

  4. Glass and shade

    Keep glazing near 30–40% of wall; shade east, west and south outside.

  5. Then size the cooling

    On the improved envelope; keep the printout and meter year one.
