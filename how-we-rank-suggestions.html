How we rank suggestions

Every Archiverse piece ends by asking you for your own proposals. Our audience is architects, engineers, educators and senior students, and their ideas deserve a fair and open evaluation. This page explains how we score them each week and what happens to the strongest.

Where suggestions come from

We read the comments on every post, on every platform. Each useful comment is filed by subject in our comment library: a proposal, a piece of evidence, an experience, a question or a counter-view. Proposals then enter the weekly evaluation below.

A comment is treated as an idea or a lead, never as a fact. Anything factual in it is verified under our Standards before we publish it.

Six criteria, scored 1 to 5

Each new proposal is scored from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong) on six criteria. Each score comes with one line of reasoning.

CriterionWhat we ask
1. Fit for PakistanDoes it suit our climate, building stock, skills, materials and culture, and the legal framework: the division of powers between federation and provinces, PEC and the building control authorities?
2. Technical feasibilityCan it be designed, built and checked with the skills, products and laboratories available here?
3. Financial feasibilityWhat is the rough cost, and who pays: the owner, the authority, an insurer, or a public–private partnership?
4. Implementation pathWho must act, which instrument changes (code, bylaw, SRO, specification or contract), and how many steps does it take? A single decision scores high; a new law scores lower.
5. Expected resultsWhat is the likely effect on lives, cost, quality or time? We judge this from a confirmed precedent elsewhere where one exists, and we never claim a result without a source.
6. Originality and valueHow much does it add beyond what has already been proposed?

Total and tie-break

The six scores add up to a total out of 30. When two proposals have the same total, the one with the higher Expected results score ranks first. All scores are our judgement and are labelled as opinion.

Implementation sketch

Every scored proposal also gets a short implementation sketch: who would act, what would change, and the first practical step. We also list what still needs verifying before the idea could be taken further.

Weekly top suggestions

Every Monday we evaluate the past week's new proposals. The three to five highest-scoring become "Top suggestions of the week", drafted for every platform. Each idea is presented as a proposal for discussion, with its score and reasoning, and with an invitation for others to build on it. Like every Archiverse post, it is approved by the founder before it goes out.

The strongest proposals can be developed further with the community: into a Code Fix discussion draft (see How we judge codes), a community episode, or the 2047 roadmap.

Credit only with permission

We name a contributor, by name or handle, only when they have given us permission. Without it, we describe them in general terms, such as "a structural engineer from Lahore", and only with their consent. We thank every contributor, named or not.
